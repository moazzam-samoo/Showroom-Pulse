# AL-TAHIR Showroom ERP - Current Capabilities & Features

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the current operational capabilities of the Al-Tahir Showroom ERP system.

## 1. Inventory & Stock Management (Inventory Screen)
- **Live Stock Tracking:** Monitor available units in real-time.
- **Detailed Bike Cards:** Interactive cards that display comprehensive vehicle details (Engine Number, Chassis Number, Model, Color) upon clicking.
- **Stock Filtering & Search:** Easily filter and search inventory by model, status (Available, Sold, Installment), or specific identifiers.
- **Add Bike Functionality:** Seamlessly log new bikes into the system with all necessary metadata.

## 2. Procurement & Dealer Management (Dealer Screen)
- **Dealer Onboarding:** Add and manage new dealers/suppliers within the system.
- **Direct Stock Purchasing:** "Add to Stock" button allows direct purchasing of bikes from specific dealers, linking the inventory directly to its source.
- **Supplier History:** Track previous purchases, financial ledgers, and transaction history with specific dealers.

## 3. Financial & Investment Management (Investment Screen)
- **Capital Management:** Add new capital to the business workflow.
- **Investment Tracking:** Monitor different investment streams and allocations.
- **Withdrawals:** Process and track capital withdrawals securely.

## 4. Point of Sale & Transactions (Sales / New Sale Screen)
- **Cash Sales:** Process direct cash purchases with instant receipt generation.
- **Installment Sales Workflow:** Comprehensive setup for installment-based sales, defining down payments, EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) amounts, duration, and guarantor details.
- **Automated Contract Generation:** Generate printable, professional contracts for customers instantly upon finalizing a sale.

## 5. Installment & Ledger Management (Installments Screen)
- **Payment Schedules:** Auto-generate dynamic payment schedules for customers.
- **Penalty Tracking:** Automatically calculate and track late fees/penalties for overdue payments.
- **Customer Ledgers:** Detailed breakdown of paid vs. remaining installments for each customer.

## 6. Executive Dashboard & Analytics (Dashboard Screen)
- **KPI Metrics:** View real-time executive data like total asset value, total active installments, and recent sales.
- **Graphical Reporting:** Visual charts displaying performance metrics and revenue breakdowns.

## 7. Advanced Reporting & Customers (Reports & Customers Screens)
- **Financial Reports:** Filterable reports tracking revenue vs. expenses to measure net profit accurately.
- **Customer Profiles:** Maintain a database of customer histories, active contracts, and past purchases.

## 8. Security, System & Utility Features
- **Hardware-Level Security (Device Licensing):** Application access is cryptographically locked to authorized Windows Motherboard UUIDs, preventing unauthorized copying or access.
- **System Notifications:** OS-level alerts reminding staff about overdue payments, system alerts, or stock shortages.
- **Data Backup & Recovery:** Built-in settings to safely export (zip) and restore the secure Isar database.
- **Invoicing & PDF Engine:** Built-in native print support for cash receipts, installment contracts, and system reports.
- **UI/UX Design:** Adaptive UI with state-of-the-art Glassmorphism elements, smooth micro-animations, and full Dark/Light mode support.

# Recommended Additions for Project Proposal

To elevate the system's value proposition for future phases, the following additions are recommended:

### Phase 1: Operational Automation
- **Barcode / QR Code Integration:** Generate QR codes for bikes to allow instant scanner-based check-in and check-out during sales.
- **AI-Powered OCR (Document Scanning):** Scan customer CNICs via webcam to auto-fill sales forms instantly.
- **Automated WhatsApp Alerts:** Send PDF receipts and installment due-date reminders directly to customers via WhatsApp.

### Phase 2: Scale & Retention
- **Service & Maintenance Tracker:** Send automated reminders to customers when their bike is due for scheduled maintenance (oil change, tuning).
- **Customer Web Portal:** A lightweight portal where customers can log in to check their remaining balance and payment history.
- **Expense & Full Accounting Module:** Track daily showroom expenses (electricity, salaries) for a true "Net Profit" calculation.
- **Multi-Branch Sync (Cloud):** A Master Admin Dashboard allowing the owner to monitor stock and sales across multiple showroom locations simultaneously.
